# GlassBox

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Confirmed: Next.js + TypeScript on Vercel, Supabase, Stripe, and an AI workflow.

## Users

George, Kathryn, and Nick building a hackathon product together.

## Product Purpose

GlassBox is the opposite of a black box: it makes agent work transparent by visualizing what agents are doing, how they reason about tasks, what informs their decisions, and where human direction fits. The goal is better understanding and human-agent alignment. The current prototype lets an agent present its approach and decisions, lets a human review and change them, and returns an approved contract to the agent. The complete product experience is still being developed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The current prototype includes human review, an inbox, an MCP connection, and agent checkpoints. It does not yet provide complete product-specific visualizations or launch-ready enforcement. Customer model, business model, pricing, and AI provider remain open. OpenAI and individual accounts are integration defaults, not fixed product requirements.
